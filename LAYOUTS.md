# Keymap diagrams

`SFT`, `CTL`, and `OPT` are one-shot modifiers. `GUI/C` holds Command and taps
Copy. `SFT/V` holds Right Shift and taps Paste. Blank cells on higher layers
fall through to the layer below.

## COLEMAK

```text
,-------------------------------------------.     ,-------------------------------------------.
| TAB |  Q  |  W  |  F  |  P  |  B  |           |  J  |  L  |  U  |  Y  |  ;  |  \  |
|-----+-----+-----+-----+-----+-----|           |-----+-----+-----+-----+-----+-----|
| CTL |  A  |  R  |  S  |  T  |  G  |           |  M  |  N  |  E  |  I  |  O  |  '  |
|-----+-----+-----+-----+-----+-----|           |-----+-----+-----+-----+-----+-----|
| SFT |  Z  |  X  |  C  |  D  |  V  |           |  K  |  H  |  ,  |  .  |  /  |SFT/V|
`-----------------------------------/           \-----------------------------------'
                  |LOWER|GUI/C| SPC |           | ENT | BSP |RAISE|
                  `-----------------'           `-----------------'
```

## Combos

```text
W + F  -> Escape       F + P  -> +           L + U  -> =
A + R  -> Tab          R + S  -> _           S + T  -> Space
N + E  -> Enter        E + I  -> -           I + O  -> Backspace
H + ,  -> :
```
